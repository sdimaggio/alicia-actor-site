About

The Story
Behind the Stage

Alicia Rignola is a New York-based actress with over three years of dedicated theatrical training. Born with a natural flair for storytelling and a Latina spirit that infuses every role she inhabits, Alicia gravitates toward complex, emotionally rich theatrical characters that demand depth, vulnerability, and fire.

A multi-talented performer, she brings both vocal and physical versatility to every project — whether commanding the stage in a dramatic scene or finding the quiet humanity in an intimate moment.
